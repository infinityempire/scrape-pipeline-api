# Compliance sources for the automated content site

## Google Search spam policies

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies

Google identifies scaled content abuse when many pages are created primarily to manipulate rankings rather than help users. The policy also describes abusive scraping as republishing or lightly transforming third-party content without original value, even if the source is cited. Affiliate pages should add meaningful value such as original information, comparisons, navigation, testing, or ratings; sponsored links should be qualified appropriately.

## Google AdSense Program policies

Source: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/48182?hl=en

Publishers must follow the program policies. They may not generate invalid clicks or impressions, encourage ad clicks, use deceptive navigation, or place ads where they can be mistaken for navigation. Google may disable ad serving or an account for policy non-compliance.

## FTC endorsement guidance

Source: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking

Where a financial connection could affect consumer evaluation, the connection should be disclosed clearly and conspicuously. Endorsements must be honest and not misleading. This is practical guidance, not a jurisdiction-specific legal opinion; site owners remain responsible for applying applicable law.
